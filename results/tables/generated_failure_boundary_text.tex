The long-horizon and persistent-stress studies define an empirical operating envelope. Across the boundary sweep, the full hybrid method remains consistently superior for packet loss below approximately $25\%$, mean one-hop delay below approximately $150$ ms, and communication partitions last fewer than about $12$ replanning steps. The transition regime begins when stale-belief accumulation and overlapping semantic events reinforce one another. Performance degradation becomes substantially steeper once packet loss approaches $35\%$, mean delay approaches $180$ ms, partitions extend to about $15$ steps, or three or more mission reinterpretation events occur within one semantic horizon. In this region, validator-approved semantic updates remain beneficial but no longer reconstruct a coherent team-level information state quickly enough to prevent repeated fallback activation and slack erosion.
